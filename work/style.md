Primary request:
Create a photorealistic [ROLE] robot that is LITERALLY CONSTRUCTED FROM REPURPOSED DECOMMISSIONED COMPANY OFFICE HARDWARE. Recognizable office components must form the robot's anatomy. Do not create a conventional futuristic robot and decorate it with office-themed details.

Family construction system:

- The face is shown on the reused display as restrained blue pixel eyes, eyebrows, and mouth.
- Audio modules are built from call-center headset earcups, conference-speaker components, or desk-phone hardware.
- The neck is made from a shortened articulated monitor arm or heavy-duty display mount with a real tilt joint and cable pass-through.
- The torso is formed from a recognizable office machine appropriate to the role: printer, scanner, conference-room controller, network appliance, badge printer, video codec, storage array, or similar enterprise hardware.
- Preserve recognizable paper trays, access doors, drive bays, vents, louvers, status LEDs, cable channels, molded plastic seams, and service fasteners.
- Shoulders and elbows use monitor-arm hinges, desk-lamp pivots, printer carriage bearings, or conference-camera mounts.
- Arms use equipment rails, cable-management channels, black corrugated sleeves, braided cables, and server or AV fasteners.
- Exactly two functional grippers are assembled from heavy-duty stapler mechanisms, binder-clip-like steel jaws, document-feeder parts, and worn black rubber pads.
- A few keyboard keys, USB ports, hub modules, or blank asset plates may be integrated as functional maintenance controls.
- Every visible component must look structurally useful rather than randomly attached.

Design intent:
An internal facilities and IT team built a dependable specialist from retired corporate equipment. Components come from two or three generations of office hardware, producing subtle mismatches in plastics, metals, fasteners, and manufacturing eras. Select panels are refinished in the role color to unify the silhouette, while enough original warm-gray, off-white, black, and metal surfaces remain visible to make the office origins unmistakable.

Surface condition:
Professionally maintained office equipment with yellowed plastic, light scuffs, fingerprints on glass, polished contact points, dust in vents, old adhesive residue from removed asset labels, and occasional replacement screws. No pervasive rust, mud, battle damage, or post-apocalyptic decay.

Composition:
Chest-up three-quarter portrait with a strong silhouette readable at small avatar size. Preserve the role prop and chest identifier. Keep the full head, both grippers, and important source-hardware details visible. Square composition and safe for circular cropping.

Lighting:
Neutral professional studio lighting that clearly reveals component origins, materials, seams, and textures. No environmental or cinematic background effects.

Background:
GENUINELY TRANSPARENT ALPHA BACKGROUND. Isolated clean cutout only. No room, scenery, gradient, floor, shadow rectangle, checkerboard pattern, or colored backdrop.

Constraints:
Clearly non-human and gender-neutral. Exactly two arms and two grippers. No generic sci-fi armor hiding the office equipment. No cartoon or animated-film style, glossy toy plastic, steampunk ornament, military styling, weapons, readable text, company logos, platform logos, or watermark.
